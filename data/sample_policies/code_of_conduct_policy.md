# Code of Conduct Policy

> **SYNTHETIC / FICTIONAL DOCUMENT.** Written for the fictional company
> "Northbridge Financial Group" to power a portfolio RAG demo. Illustrative
> internal policy language only.

**Document ID:** COC-POL-007
**Owner:** Ethics & Compliance Office
**Effective Date:** 2025-01-02 (fictional)
**Review Cycle:** Annual

## Section 1: Purpose

This Code of Conduct sets the minimum standard of ethical behavior expected
of every Northbridge Financial Group employee, contractor, and agent acting
on the company's behalf.

## Section 2: Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any personal, financial, or familial relationship
that could reasonably be seen to influence a business decision, including
relationships with vendors under consideration by Third-Party Risk
Management. Disclosures are made through the annual attestation process or
immediately upon a material change.

## Section 3: Use of AI Tools

Employees using AI-assisted tools, including internal copilots and
approved external generative AI products, must:

1. Never submit Confidential or Restricted data (per the Data
   Classification Standard, DCS-STD-004) to a tool not explicitly approved
   for that data tier
2. Treat AI-generated output as a draft requiring human review before it is
   relied upon for a customer communication, regulatory filing, or
   financial decision
3. Not represent AI-generated content as independently human-verified fact
   without performing that verification
4. Report suspected AI system malfunction, hallucination in a
   consequential context, or attempted prompt injection to Information
   Security

## Section 4: Confidentiality

Employees must protect confidential business, customer, and employee
information both during and after their employment or engagement, consistent
with the Data Classification Standard and Information Security Access
Control Policy.

## Section 5: Reporting Violations

Employees are required to report suspected violations of this Code through
the Ethics Hotline or directly to Compliance. Retaliation against a
good-faith reporter is strictly prohibited and is itself a violation subject
to disciplinary action.

## Section 6: Consequences of Violation

Violations of this Code may result in disciplinary action up to and
including termination of employment, and referral to regulatory or law
enforcement authorities where warranted.
