# Customer Due Diligence Standard

> **SYNTHETIC / FICTIONAL DOCUMENT.** Written for the fictional company
> "Northbridge Financial Group" to power a portfolio RAG demo. This is
> illustrative internal policy language only — it does not quote or
> represent any real law, regulation, or actual institution's standard.

**Document ID:** CDD-STD-002
**Owner:** Financial Crimes Compliance
**Effective Date:** 2025-02-01 (fictional)
**Review Cycle:** Annual

## Section 1: Purpose

This standard defines the minimum customer due diligence (CDD) procedures
required before Northbridge Financial Group establishes or continues a
business relationship with a customer, in support of the enterprise
financial crimes risk management program.

## Section 2: Customer Risk Rating

Each customer must receive a risk rating of Low, Medium, or High at
onboarding, derived from factors including:

- Customer type (individual, small business, corporate, correspondent)
- Geographic footprint and jurisdictions of operation
- Product and service usage patterns
- Expected transaction volume and velocity
- Beneficial ownership complexity

High-risk customers require Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD), described in
Section 4.

## Section 3: Standard Due Diligence Elements

For all customers, Standard Due Diligence requires, at minimum:

1. Verification of legal name, date of birth or formation, and address
2. Identification and verification of beneficial owners holding 25% or
   greater ownership interest
3. Understanding of the nature and purpose of the customer relationship
4. Screening against applicable sanctions and watchlists at onboarding and
   on an ongoing basis

## Section 4: Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)

Enhanced Due Diligence applies to customers rated High risk and must
include, in addition to Standard Due Diligence elements:

- Source of wealth and source of funds documentation
- Senior management approval prior to onboarding
- More frequent periodic reviews (at minimum every 12 months, versus 36
  months for Low risk customers)
- Enhanced transaction monitoring thresholds

## Section 5: Ongoing Monitoring and Periodic Review

Customer risk ratings are not static. A triggering event (material change
in ownership, adverse media, unusual transaction pattern) requires an
out-of-cycle review. Automated monitoring systems, including any AI-assisted
alert generation or alert triage tooling, must maintain a documented
methodology and be subject to independent model validation per the Model
Risk Management Policy (MRM-POL-001).

## Section 6: Recordkeeping

CDD records, including risk rating rationale and supporting documentation,
must be retained for a minimum of five years following account closure, or
longer if required by an applicable legal hold.
